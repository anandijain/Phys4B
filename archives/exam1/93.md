# problem
We have a positive plate at y=d and a negative plate at y=0
the plates are assumed to create a uniform E field and have length L 

We have a charged mass(charge=-q, mass=m, v_initial=[vi, 0])

We want to find the potential across the plates to cause the charged mass to pass through the point (L, d)

# soln

firstly, we find how long its going to take the particle to go thru (L, 0) with just horizontal velocity

x0 + vi(t)t + 1/2 ax(t) t2 = L
t = L/vi

So now we know how much time we have to make the particle travel d vertically 

F = ma (sys = charged mass)
qE = m a
a = q E / m

For a field E, we can find the potential difference with a line integral across the plates 

Delta V = -int(0, d) E dot dl   | NOTE here E's direction and dl are opposite so we need to incur another - sign
    = -int(0, d) - E dy
    = E int(0, d)dy
    = E d
so E = Delta V / d
so a = q Delta V / (m d) 

we can apply the kinematics equation again

y0 + vi_y(t)*t + 1/2 a(t) t2 = d 
1/2 a (L2/vi2)= d 
a = 2 vi2 d / L2
q Delta V / (m d) =  2 vi2 d / L2
Delta V = 2 m d2 vi2 / (L2 q)
