# problem

2 conducting spheres (radii, R1, R2) are connected together 

find the ratio of charge density on them

# soln 

because they are spheres whe know that the potentials will be equal and 
each will be Vi = k Qi / Ri

the surface charge density will be sig_i = Qi / (4 pi Ri2)

so Qi = sig_i * 4 pi Ri2

k Q1 / R1 = k Q2 / R2

sig1 * 4 pi (R1)2 / R1 = sig2 * 4 pi (R2)2 / R2
sig1 / sig2 = R2 / R1
