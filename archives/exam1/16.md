# problem. 

dielectric sphere (unknown radius) non uniform volume charge density (radial)

rho(r) = p(1-r/r0) where p and r0 constants

find r such that |E| = 0

# soln

let x be the unknown distance from the center such that E = 0
cint E dot dA = qenc / eps
since sphere symmetry E is radial field and then its magnitude is constant for fixed r
thus
E * 4 pi x2 = qenc / eps 

qenc = int(0, x) rho(r) dVol 
want 
0 = 
    = int(0, x) rho(r) dVol 
    = int(0, x) rho(r) 4 pi r2 dr
    = int(0, x) p(1-r/r0) 4 pi r2 dr
    = 4 pi p int(0, x) r2 - r3/r0 dr
    = 4 pi p [r3/3 - r4/(4r0) @ (0, x)]
    = 4 pi p (x3/3 - x4/(4r0))
    = x3 (1/3 - x/(4r0))
x = {0, 4/3 r0}
