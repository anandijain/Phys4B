# problem

we are given two equal opposite charges
q1 = q and q2 = -q both of mass m 
they hang from strings and are distance ri apart both initially at rest 
the strings make an angle theta_i from vertical
there is gravity in this problem. 

the charges come together (colliding) and eliminate. perfectly elastic 

find the maximum angle made after collision.

# solution

W = Delta U (sys = q1, q2, earth)

we will have 1 potential for each pair of bodies (ignoring the gravitational attraction of the q1 and q2)
the first two are gravity 
Delta U(g1) + Delta U(g2) + Delta Ue(1,2)

We have 1 kinetic energy for each body also but they all start and end at rest so all Delta KE = 0

while there is an external force acting on the fixed planet earth (which would otherwise move if not held fixed), there is no displacement of earth, thus W = 0 by dl=0

0 = Delta U(g1) + Delta U(g2) + Delta Ue(1,2)

The gravitational potential energy changes are

mg(delta(y)) by symmetry these become 
2m g (yf - yi)

then electric potential energy is k q1 q2 / r 
in our case will be -k q2 / r. minus since one is negative charged and they have equal magnitude hence the q2

The initial Ue = -k q2 / ri and the final is 0 since they eliminate 

then Delta Ue = k q2 / ri       | so it gained electric potential energy? its as if we let them fly to infinity 

0 = 2m g (yf - yi) + k q2 / ri      | but ri = 2L sin theta_i 
    = 2m g (yf - yi) + k q2 / (2L sin theta)

but what is yf - yi. 

if you look L - L cos theta is the distance from the bottom of the pendulum 
its helpful to consider this to by y = 0 then you can see that L - L cos is the absolute height of the charges 
so (L - L cos alpha) - (L - L cos theta)
= L(cos(theta) - cos(alpha))

    = 2m g L(cos(theta) - cos(alpha)) + k q2 / (2L sin theta)

now we solve for alpha


2m g L(cos(theta) - cos(alpha)) = -k q2 / (2L sin theta)
cos(alpha) = k q2 / (4m g L^2 sin theta) + cos(theta)
alpha = acos(k q2 / (4m g L^2 sin theta) + cos(theta))


We know that alpha < theta_i because the electric potential energy increased and that must have come from the gravitational potential energy as all Delta KE was 0.
